# Lundburgerr authored campaign → maps → Tangjung

Current level40 uses authored Act3: 35 common +9/9 specialization =44 ordinary, plus4 paid ascendancy and free Kitava start/Masterwork. Level40 gives39 ordinary before actually claimed rewards. Full tree needs at least5 claimed ordinary reward points,9 specialization capacity and the actual ascendancy points.
Interludes is85 ordinary; Maps109 is a later target, not an instruction to spend109 upon first mapping. Follow connected source-only prefixes as points are earned.
Normal body armour is mandatory while Masterwork is allocated; Blood Rush converts6% mana costs to life. Verify both resource recovery and actual gear/gem attributes. No HC or DPS PASS.

Raw author exports are preserved in lundburgerr_authored/raw_native and author_download.raw.zip. Display1..100 is not acquisition eligibility.

Make sure to use armourer scraps on your shield, you get most of them back if you salvage the shield when you find an upgrade. Every 5% quality will give you one armourer scrap from salvage.


descriptionPoeSkillGems:
Guaranteed lesser jewelers orb in Sandswept marsh, At the end of the zone there is a small area with 2 rare monsters and a chest. Kill the rares and loot the chest for the lesser jewelers orb, save it for Fortifying cry.
You will first cut Fortifying Cry then Sunder from your first two lvl 9+ skill gems.
Your skill slots will be full, so you can slot out Leap Slam temporarily until we can remove Freezing Mark in Act 4.
T3 support gems should be cut in this order:
If you don't already have extra of these supports, cut them for Fortifying Cry:
1. Raging Cry
2. Fire Attunement
Then cut these:
1. Close Combat I (For Fortifying Cry)
2. Magnified Area II (Shield Wall)
3. Magnified Area II (Herald of Ash)
4. Prolonged Duration II (Sunder)
5. Rapid Attacks I (Sunder)
6. Font of Mana (Shockwave Totem), Prolonged Duration II if you already have mana leech on gear.
If you drop extra lesser jewelers orbs, put it on Shockwave Totem and then Infernal Cry first.
Spirit gems:
After killing the boss in Azak you will get 30 extra spirit. You can either cut Attrition or War Banner,  your choice. War Banner is typically more damage on bosses but it means you have to actively put down a banner.


IMPORTANT:
Make sure that that the warcries are on weapon set 2 (assuming you allocated the warcry nodes on weapon set 2) and everything else is on weapon set 1.


descriptionPoe2PassiveTree:

But Shield wall warrior is strong enough to skip this and just get the second and third ascend at the same time when doing a lvl 60 Barya. Author notes:

overview:
Shield Wall is not unlocked until lvl 22 unfortunately, so before that we will use Rolling slam + Boneshatter to clear and Perfect Strike after lvl 14 for Single target. Aim to get to lvl 14 right before fighting Count Geonor. Make sure you Talked to Leitis after killing the Executioner, she will then give you a guaranteed lvl 5 skill gem in town that you can use to cut Perfect Strike.
Rolling Slam + Boneshatter is a strong combo, but can take a little bit of practice to master.
Rolling slam has two slams built in, but you want to only do the first slam, then cancel the second slam by using Boneshatter before it. You can also control the rolling slam mid-attack by moving your mouse, which is sometimes useful, especially when rolling into a dangerous pack. You can then rolling slam into them from an angle to avoid getting hit by a bunch of attacks.
You can use a talisman on your swap if you want, but it's not super necessary, it will enable the usage of Pounce which helps you traverse the zones quicker. But this is more of a speedrunning tactic.


descriptionPoeSkillGems:
Keep your Rolling Slam at lvl 1! If you're forced to upgrade your Rolling Slam, make sure to keep the lvl 1 copy and don't upgrade it. If you need more stun buildup to prime enemies for stun for Boneshatter, you can always cut Brink I in act 1 in next league.
Support gem priority
1. Impact Shockwave (Boneshatter)
2. Concentrated Area (Mace Strike)
3. Fire Attunement (Mace Strike)
4. Magnified Area I (Boneshatter)
5. Tireless (Infernal Cry)
6. Window of Opportunity I (Perfect Strike)
7. Magnified Area I (Herald of Ash)
8. Rapid Attacks I (Rolling Slam)
Optional. Brink I (Rolling Slam)
If you have too high damage or stun build up, Rolling Slam might stun the enemies immediately which you don't want. You just want to have enough stun buildup so that you can proc the shockwave on Boneshatter. Enemies will have a white glowing orb on them when they have just the right amount of Stun buildup.
When you get Perfect Strike move Concentrated Area from Mace Strike to Perfect Strike. If you have issues timing and hitting with your Perfect Strike on bosses, you can slot in either Fire Attunement or Rapid Attacks I (Or both) instead of Window of Opportunity I or Concentrated Area.
