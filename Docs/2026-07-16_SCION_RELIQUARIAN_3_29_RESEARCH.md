## Important Reliquarian Hooks

### Direct Skill Engines

- Fidelitas' Spike: Hits always Shock; deal no Physical Damage; Herald of Thunder has 100% increased Buff Effect; Herald of Thunder has 100% increased Mana Reservation Efficiency.
- Ngamahu's Flame: Trigger Level 20 Molten Burst on Melee Hit; Damage Penetrates 20% Fire Resistance.
- Arakaali's Fang: Trigger Level 20 Raise Spiders on Kill.
- The Black Cane: Each Summoned Phantasm grants Phantasmal Might.
- Gloomfang: Skills Chain +1 times; Projectiles that have chained gain 20% of Non-Chaos Damage as extra Chaos Damage.
- The Sacred Chalice: Flask charge on crit plus Physical as Extra Cold/Fire/Lightning based on recently used resistance flasks.

### Defensive / Sustain Hooks

- Dawnbreaker: 25% Cold, Lightning, and Physical Damage taken as Fire Damage.
- Kaom's Roots: +100 maximum Life; cannot be Knocked Back, Evaded, Stunned; action speed cannot be modified below base.
- Presence of Chayula: Cannot be Stunned; 20% of Maximum Life converted to Energy Shield.
- Xirgil's Crank: +1 to all Spell Skill Gems; 30% chance for Energy Shield Recharge to start when you Block.
- The Apostate: removes all ES; gain maximum Life instead of maximum ES from equipped armour; -12 maximum Life per level. Strong but not automatically good because the life penalty is large.
- Melding of the Flesh: -5% all maximum Elemental Resistances; Elemental Resistance capped by highest maximum Elemental Resistance instead.

## Candidate Ranking For This Goal

### 1. Scion Reliquarian Herald of Thunder Autobomber

Why it matters:
- Fidelitas' Spike is the cleanest direct node for the user's autobomber goal.
- Existing Herald of Thunder autobomber logic is already established: HoT creates storms, Storm Secret can double hit frequency and help boss viability, and HoT damage scales with generic elemental/lightning rather than spell damage.

Fit:
- Mapping: high
- Guardian maps: medium, depends on Storm Secret / shock loop / single target
- Delve/Fossil: medium, needs Dawnbreaker/Chayula/Kaom defensive package
- Starter safety: medium-low until gear loop is online

Research verdict:
- Top project candidate. Not necessarily Day 1 safest, but most aligned with Scion Reliquarian.

References:
- https://odealo.com/articles/herald-of-thunder-poison-auto-bomber-build

### 2. Scion Reliquarian Static Strike / Ngamahu / Melee Autobomber

Why it matters:
- Static Strike already has an autobomber-like loop: hit once, maintain static energy, beams hit nearby enemies repeatedly.
- Ngamahu's Flame node gives Level 20 Molten Burst on melee hit and Fire Penetration, so a melee-hit shell is plausible.

Key risk:
- Forum discussion points out Reliquarian-triggered Molten Burst may not be supportable by support gems. If true, it is a supplemental trigger, not main DPS.
- Ngamahu/Molten Burst also needs conversion checked. If Molten Burst's damage is not fire by default in this implementation, Fire Penetration may require manual conversion to matter.

Fit:
- Mapping: medium-high
- Guardian maps: medium, needs PoB confirmation
- Delve/Fossil: medium-high if built tanky
- Starter safety: medium

Research verdict:
- High-fun experiment. Do not lock this as the only plan until PoB confirms support/conversion/scaling.

### 3. Scion Reliquarian Gloomfang Bow

Why it matters:
- Gloomfang node directly rewards chained projectiles, which can map well with Lightning Arrow / Tornado Shot / other chain bow skills.
- Bow characters are proven high-speed farmers, but Deadeye normally carries this archetype. Scion must compensate with Reliquarian defense and item power.

Fit:
- Mapping: high
- Guardian maps: medium
- Delve/Fossil: low-medium unless defenses are solved
- Starter safety: medium-low due bow gear dependence

Research verdict:
- Good speed-farming fallback if Gloomfang node is strong in PoB. Not the best Delve first plan.

References:
- https://www.poe-vault.com/guides/lightning-arrow-deadeye-build-guide

### 4. Scion Reliquarian Black Cane / Arakaali / Spell-Minion Hybrid

Why it matters:
- Top cluster has Arakaali's Fang and The Black Cane hooks. The Black Cane/Phantasmal Might scales spells with added physical damage from phantasms.
- This pushes toward physical spells or spell-hit hybrids more than pure minion scaling.

Key risk:
- Raise Spiders from the ascendancy may not be supportable like an actual Arakaali's Fang socket setup, so it may be utility/poison/attack-speed support rather than full minion main DPS.

Fit:
- Mapping: medium
- Guardian maps: medium-high if spell setup scales
- Delve/Fossil: high if ES/block/Chayula/Xirgil layers work
- Starter safety: unknown

Research verdict:
- Best "research lab" candidate after HoT. Needs PoB prototype before committing.

- https://poedb.tw/us/Arakaalis_Fang

### 5. Scion Reliquarian Blight of Contagion

Why it matters:
- Blight of Contagion is proven as a low-budget chaos DoT mapper and ignores many map-mod problems.
- It has good Delve/Fossil comfort and can do Guardian maps, but not as a boss-rushing specialist.

Key issue:
- Reliquarian has no direct chaos DoT node. Scion Blight is a fallback/safety shell, not a direct abuse case.

Fit:
- Mapping: high
- Guardian maps: medium-low speed, medium reliability
- Delve/Fossil: high
- Starter safety: medium-high

Research verdict:
- Best conservative fallback if Scion must be started but Reliquarian experiments fail. Trickster remains the safer non-Scion version.
